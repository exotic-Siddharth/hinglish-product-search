🛒 Hinglish Product Search

Type a shopping query in Hinglish, romanised Hindi mixed with English (gents ke liye kaala jacket). The page rewrites the Hindi words it knows back to English, then searches 40,000 Amazon products. It shows the results with and without the rewrite, so you can see what the rewrite fixes. Everything runs in your browser.

How it works

  • Rewrite: a dictionary built from a 185-word Hinglish lexicon, with every spelling variant (kaala / kala, safed / sufed), maps known Hindi words back to English. Grammar words become English too (ke liye → for, se kam → under), and fillers like chahiye or dikhao are dropped.
  • Search: BM25 keyword search over each product's title, brand and colour, with plural stemming so shoe matches shoes.
  • Labels: for the example queries, each result shows Amazon's relevance judgment from the ESCI dataset: Exact Substitute Complement Irrelevant, or not judged if it was judged only for other queries.

  • With no model at all, the rewrite closes most of the gap to English on generated queries (0.360 against 0.376) and about half of it on free-form queries.
  • Hindi words outside the dictionary pass through unchanged, and that is where search still fails: the evaluation's "unseen words" set is not helped by the rewrite.
  • The free-form queries were written by an LLM, not by real shoppers. Half of their Hindi words are not in the dictionary.
